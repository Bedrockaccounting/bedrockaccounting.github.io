CIC34 director questionnaire

Your answers help us write your CIC Report (CIC34), which is filed with your accounts. Use your own words. We will turn them into the formal wording. Questions marked * must be answered.

Part 1: What you did this year and who it helped

Tell us what the company actually did during the year and how it helped your community. Real numbers and examples make the report stronger.

Example: "Ran 48 weekly lunch clubs in two halls, and 6 summer day trips."

Example: "Older people living alone in Slough, and their carers."

A rough figure is fine. Include volunteers if relevant.

Example: "Members say they feel less lonely. 20 people were linked to local health services."

Part 2: Listening to the people you serve

A "stakeholder" is anyone affected by your work: the people you help, volunteers, funders, partners or the local council.

Part 3: Directors' pay

Paying back genuine out-of-pocket expenses (with receipts) is not pay. Anything else is: salary, fees, consultancy, benefits.

1. Did any director receive pay, fees or benefits from the company this year? *

Name, what it was for, and the amount for the year.

Example: "Jane Smith, project manager salary, £12,000."

Part 4: Giving away money or assets

This means giving money, equipment or property to another organisation free or below its value. For example, a grant to a charity, or donating your old minibus to another CIC.

1. Did the company give away any money or assets for free or below their value this year? *

What was given, who received it, and its value (an estimate is fine).

Example: "Donated 5 laptops worth about £1,000 to Riverside Youth CIC."
