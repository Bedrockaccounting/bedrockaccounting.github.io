Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

This notice explains how Bedrock Accounting Services Ltd uses the details you send us through this website. We are the data controller for those details.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry, we collect your name, email address, phone number (if you give it), organisation name and type, the service you are asking about and your message.

Why we use it

We use your details only to reply to your enquiry and, if you ask, to give you a quote. We do not sell your details, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form, and our legitimate interest in replying to people who contact us.

Who else sees it

Our enquiry form is run by Formspree, which passes your message to us by email. Formspree may store your details on servers outside the UK, with safeguards in place. We do not share your details with anyone else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

If you become a client, we keep your details as part of your client file. If you do not, we delete your enquiry within 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or withdraw your consent at any time. To do this, email francisca@bedrockaccountingservices.co.uk or call 07426 145239.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your details, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
